# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in native_sqlite to be safe, respectful, and welcoming
for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect differing viewpoints and acknowledge mistakes.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid harassment, threats, discrimination,
  sexualized conduct, or publishing another person's private information.
- Protect credentials, private database data, and other sensitive material
  encountered while debugging.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other
participation that violates these expectations. Depending on severity or
recurrence, maintainers may issue a warning, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban a participant.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through their
GitHub profiles. Do not post sensitive reports in a public issue. Maintainers
will limit disclosure to the people needed to review the report and will avoid
conflicts of interest when assigning a reviewer.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is officially
representing the project elsewhere.
